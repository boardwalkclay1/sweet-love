Back on the Beltline

The day was warm, the sky was soft, and the Beltline felt like a movie set. I was gliding on my longboard, just another day, just another ride…

And then I saw you—kneeling to lace up your roller skates. The world didn’t just slow down, it stopped. It was like the universe said, “Look. Remember this.”

Love at First Sight

You stood up and started rolling, and I swear, you didn’t just move—you floated. The sun hit your face, your hair, your smile, and I felt something in my chest shift.

I didn’t know your name. I didn’t know your story. But I knew this: I was looking at the most beautiful person I had ever seen.

When we went our separate ways that day, I honestly thought I’d never see you again.

Falling in Love Twice

Time passed. Life kept moving. And then one day, I met you again—different place, different moment, same soul.

I didn’t recognize you as the girl from the Beltline. I just knew that my heart was doing that same thing again— that quiet, stunned, “oh no, I’m falling” feeling.

I fell in love with you for the second time, without knowing you were the same girl I’d fallen for the first time.

That has never happened to me before. And now, every time I see you, I fall in love with you all over again.

Right Here, Right Now

Today, I’m not just thinking about how we met. I’m thinking about how grateful I am that you’re still here.

The girl on skates became the woman I lean on, laugh with, dream with. You’re not just in my life—you’re the center of it.

My Anchor in the Storm

There were moments when everything felt heavy— when life was loud, confusing, and painful.

You were the reason I made it through some of the hardest times in my life. You didn’t just stand next to me—you held me up.

You made the worst days softer. You made the darkest nights feel survivable.

My Angel

You are an angel in my life. You turned struggle into growth, loneliness into partnership, and fear into something I could face with you beside me.

I love you more than I can ever fully say. And I am so, so grateful that I get to love you in this lifetime.

Our Land, Our Freedom

When I think about our future, I see land—ours. I see us waking up where the air is quiet and the sky feels close.

I see us traveling wherever and whenever we want, moving through the world with ease, joy, and freedom.

All of it growing from that first moment on the Beltline, and every moment we’ve shared since.

Home in the Woods

I see us living in the woods, comfortably, surrounded by trees, sky, and quiet.

Technology when we want it, nature when we need it, and love holding it all together.

Spiritual, clean, pure, grounded— with our ancestors watching over us, proud of what we built.

The House I’m Building for You

I’m building you a house— not just with wood and walls, but with intention, love, and every lesson we’ve lived.

One day we’ll sit on our land, look back at the Beltline, the present, the storms, the laughter— and know it was all worth it.

Because it led me to you, and kept me with you. I’m so glad I met you. I’m so glad I get to love you— in the past, in the present, and in every future we build.
