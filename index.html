The Day Everything Changed

It was a warm day on the Beltline… I was rolling smooth on my longboard, mind on the wind, when I saw you—kneeling to lace up your roller skates.

The sun hit you like a spotlight. And right then, I swear… I thought you were the most beautiful thing in the world.

Where do you want to go, my love?

Our story stretches across time. Choose a chapter of us.

Back on the Beltline

I remember the sound of my wheels on the concrete, the hum of the city, the warmth of the sun. And then there you were—bending down, lacing up your roller skates, like the world had paused just to frame you in that moment.

I didn’t know your name. I just knew my heart had found something it didn’t want to forget.

Love at First Sight

As you stood up and rolled forward, I watched you glide like you’d always belonged there. I fell in love with you right then—before a word, before a hello. Just you, the sun, the skates, and my longboard heart trying to keep up.

When we parted ways that day, I honestly thought I’d never see you again.

Falling in Love Twice

Life moved on… or at least I thought it did. Then one day, I met you again—different place, different time. I had no idea you were the same girl from the Beltline.

And somehow, I fell in love with you all over again. That has never happened to me before— to fall in love with the same soul twice, without even knowing it was you both times.

Now, every time I see you, I fall in love with you again. Over and over. Like it’s the first time.

Right Here, Right Now

Today, I’m just grateful. Grateful that somehow, the universe brought you back into my life. Grateful that the girl on skates became the woman I get to love.

You’re not just part of my life—you’re the heart of it.

My Anchor in the Storm

There were times I didn’t know how I was going to make it through. Times when everything felt heavy, dark, and impossible.

And you— you were the reason I got through some of the most difficult moments of my life. You made the weight lighter. You made the days softer. You made the pain bearable just by being there.

My Angel

You are an angel in my life. You turned struggle into growth, loneliness into partnership, and fear into something I could face with you by my side.

I love you more than I can ever fully say. And I am so, so grateful that I get to love you in this lifetime.

Our Land, Our Freedom

When I think about our future, I see land—ours. I see us traveling wherever and whenever we want, moving through the world with ease, joy, and freedom.

All of it growing from that first moment on the Beltline, and every moment we’ve shared since.

Home in the Woods

I see us living in the woods, comfortably, surrounded by trees, sky, and quiet. Technology when we want it, nature when we need it, and love holding it all together.

Spiritual, clean, pure, grounded— with our ancestors watching over us, proud.

The House I’m Building for You

I’m building you a house— not just with wood and walls, but with intention, love, and every lesson we’ve lived.

One day we’ll sit on our land, look back at the Beltline, the present, the storms, the laughter— and know it was all worth it. Because it led me to you, and kept me with you.

I’m so glad I met you. I’m so glad I get to love you— in the past, in the present, and in every future we build.
