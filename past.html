Back on the Beltline

The day was warm, the sky was soft, and the Beltline felt like a movie set. I was gliding on my longboard, just another day, just another ride…

And then I saw you—kneeling to lace up your roller skates. The world didn’t just slow down, it stopped. It was like the universe said, “Look. Remember this.”

Love at First Sight

You stood up and started rolling, and I swear, you didn’t just move—you floated. The sun hit your face, your hair, your smile, and I felt something in my chest shift.

I didn’t know your name. I didn’t know your story. But I knew this: I was looking at the most beautiful person I had ever seen.

When we went our separate ways that day, I honestly thought I’d never see you again.

Falling in Love Twice

Time passed. Life kept moving. And then one day, I met you again—different place, different moment, same soul.

I didn’t recognize you as the girl from the Beltline. I just knew that my heart was doing that same thing again— that quiet, stunned, “oh no, I’m falling” feeling.

I fell in love with you for the second time, without knowing you were the same girl I’d fallen for the first time.

Now, every time I see you, I fall in love with you all over again.
