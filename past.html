Back on the Beltline

The day was warm, the sky was soft, and the Beltline felt like a movie set. I was gliding on my longboard, just another day, just another ride…

And then I saw you—kneeling to lace up your roller skates. The world didn’t just slow down, it stopped.

Love at First Sight

You stood up and started rolling, and I swear, you didn’t just move—you floated. The sun hit your face, your hair, your smile, and I felt something in my chest shift.

I didn’t know your name. I didn’t know your story. But I knew I was looking at the most beautiful person I had ever seen.

Falling in Love Twice

I fell in love with you for the second time without knowing you were the same girl from the Beltline.

And now, every time I see you, I fall in love with you all over again.
