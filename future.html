Our Land

When I think about our future, I see land—ours. A place where mornings feel soft, quiet, and sacred. A place where we wake up slow, wrapped in peace instead of pressure.

I see us stepping outside barefoot, breathing air that belongs to us, standing on ground that holds our story.

Our Peace

I see us living in the woods, comfortably— surrounded by trees, sky, and quiet that feels like medicine.

Technology when we want it. Nature when we need it. Love holding it all together.

I see us cooking together, laughing together, building routines that feel like home.

Our Adventures

I see us traveling— not rushing, not running, but moving through the world with ease and freedom.

I see us exploring new cities, new forests, new oceans, hand in hand, heart in heart.

I see us creating memories that feel like scenes from a movie we get to live inside forever.

The Home I’m Building for You

I’m building you a home— not just with wood and walls, but with intention, love, and every lesson we’ve lived.

One day we’ll sit on our land, look back at the Beltline, the storms, the laughter, the growth— and know it was all worth it.

Because it led me to you, and it kept me with you.
